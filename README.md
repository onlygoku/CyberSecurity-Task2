# CyberSecurity-Task2  
## Phishing Email Analysis

This repository contains a cybersecurity analysis report and supporting evidence from examining a phishing email. The goal of this task was to identify spoofing techniques, social engineering tactics, and other malicious indicators commonly used in phishing attacks.

---

## Objective

The objective of this task was to analyze a sample phishing email and identify its malicious characteristics.  
This exercise helps develop practical skills in:

- Email threat detection
- Recognizing phishing indicators
- Understanding attacker social engineering techniques
- Performing basic incident analysis and reporting

---

## Analysis Process

1. A sample phishing email (fake PayPal security alert) was obtained from an online source for analysis.
2. The email content was carefully reviewed to identify suspicious elements.
3. Multiple phishing indicators were examined, including:
   - Sender email address spoofing
   - Use of urgent or threatening language
   - Spelling and grammatical errors
   - Suspicious or misleading links
4. Findings were documented and compiled into a structured report.
